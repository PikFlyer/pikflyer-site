Privacy Policy隱私權政策

Last updated: July 18, 2026最後更新：2026 年 7 月 18 日

1. Information We Collect1. 我們收集的資訊

When you purchase a subscription, our payment partner (Creem) collects your name, email address, and payment information. We receive your email address, order status, and license key for account management and support. We do not collect or store your payment card details directly.當您購買訂閱時，我們的支付夥伴（Creem）會收集您的姓名、電子郵件和付款資訊。我們僅接收您的電子郵件、訂單狀態和授權金鑰，用於帳戶管理與客服。我們不直接收集或儲存您的信用卡資訊。

2. How We Use Your Information2. 資訊使用方式

We use your email address solely to manage your subscription, send license keys, and provide customer support. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes.我們僅使用您的電子郵件來管理訂閱、發送授權金鑰和提供客戶支援。我們不會出售、出租或分享您的個人資訊給任何第三方作行銷用途。

3. Data the Software Collects3. 軟體收集的資料

Pik Flyer runs locally on your computer or Android phone. The Software does not upload your GPS coordinates, location history, route history, or POI search history to us. Location simulation happens on your own device. For license enforcement, the Software may generate a salted hash of a device identifier; the raw hardware identifier is not sent to us.Pik Flyer 在您的電腦或 Android 手機本機運行。軟體不會將 GPS 座標、位置歷史、路線歷史或 POI 搜尋紀錄上傳給我們。定位模擬在您的裝置本機進行。為了授權管理，軟體可能產生裝置識別資訊的 salted hash；原始硬體識別碼不會傳送給我們。

4. License Verification4. 授權驗證

The Software periodically contacts our licensing endpoint or Creem to verify your subscription status. This request may include your license key, app version, platform, and salted device hash. It does not include GPS coordinates, route history, or third-party app activity.軟體會定期聯繫我們的授權端點或 Creem，以驗證您的訂閱狀態。此請求可能包含授權金鑰、App 版本、平台與 salted device hash；不包含 GPS 座標、路線歷史或第三方 App 使用活動。

5. Cookies and Tracking5. Cookie 與追蹤

Our website uses first-party aggregate counters for page views and APK downloads. We do not use cookies or third-party tracking scripts.我們的網站只使用第一方匿名總數統計瀏覽與 APK 下載次數；不使用 Cookie 或第三方追蹤腳本。

6. Data Security6. 資料安全

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. All communications between the Software and our licensing server use HTTPS encryption. Local license data is stored using platform security features where available, such as macOS Keychain, Windows credential protection, or Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage.我們採取合理措施保護您的資訊。軟體與授權伺服器之間的所有通訊均使用 HTTPS 加密。本機授權資料會盡可能使用平台安全功能儲存，例如 macOS Keychain、Windows 認證保護或 Android Keystore 支援的加密儲存。

7. Data Retention7. 資料保留

If you cancel your subscription, we retain your email and license information for up to 12 months for support purposes, after which it is deleted.如果您取消訂閱，我們會保留您的電子郵件和授權資訊最多 12 個月以供支援用途，之後將予以刪除。

8. Your Rights8. 您的權利

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. We will respond to your request within 30 days.您可隨時要求存取、更正或刪除您的個人資料。我們將在 30 天內回覆您的請求。

9. Children's Privacy9. 兒童隱私

Pik Flyer is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.Pik Flyer 不適用於 13 歲以下兒童。我們不會故意收集兒童的個人資訊。

10. Changes to This Policy10. 政策變更

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.我們可能會不時更新本隱私權政策。變更將在本頁面公佈並更新日期。

11. Contact11. 聯絡方式

For privacy-related questions, contact us at support@pikflyer.app.隱私相關問題請聯絡 support@pikflyer.app。
